1. Testing Perception and Processing

Everything that happens in the body — physically and emotionally — is reflected in the brain, the body's primary regulatory system. These processes shape a unique internal "filter" through which we perceive the world. iris uses a specialized test that decodes this perceptual filter by evaluating how the user reconstructs color from visual memory. This test reveals the brain’s current configuration and its control patterns over bodily systems.

2. Session Process

Each iris session begins with a color perception task. You view a short color-rich animation, then reconstruct its color pattern from memory. This process reflects nervous system regulation and emotional imprinting — read without words, story, or symptom. The results reveal the state of internal integration and map psycho-physiological coherence or dissonance.

3. Personalized Spectrum Prescription

After testing, iris prescribes a light-coded session calibrated to your result — a visual sequence that speaks directly to your nervous system through the eye. The spectral therapy is not symbolic; it is math-based and physiologically precise. As a result, the qualitative interconnection of all body systems is encouraged and restored, supporting improved emotional and physical wellbeing.

4. Session Types

• Core Reset — Burnout, overload, emotional fog
• Focus & Stability — Overstimulation, restlessness, ADHD
• Dream Clarity — Sleep, subconscious blocks, grief or visioning
• Custom Ritual — Tailored Spiral-coded spectral session guided by your field

5. Evaluation & Ongoing Adaptation

Testing may be repeated 1.5–2 months after the first session block to assess adaptation. Final testing is recommended at the end of each therapeutic cycle. These assessments help track how the nervous system has integrated the therapy and whether new support patterns are needed. Adjustments can also be made to address acute stress, infections, or unexpected events.

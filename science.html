iris — the science of color memory

Perception as Diagnostic

iris uses a process where a viewer watches a color-rich visual. The same visual is then stripped of its color. The participant reconstructs what they believe to be the original colors. This reconstruction is interpreted using complex mathematical models that analyze the psycho-emotional and physiological condition of the viewer.

Why Color?

The eye is a direct interface to the nervous system. Light enters not just biologically, but neurologically — shaping rhythm, emotion, cognition, and memory. By assessing how one remembers and rebuilds color, we map perception distortions and internal field tensions without requiring verbal input or story.

Sessions & Personalized Spectral Therapy

After assessment, iris prescribes individualized color-light sequences. These are not aesthetic — they are mathematically calculated, time-coded therapies delivered through the eye to calm the nervous system and restore internal coherence.
